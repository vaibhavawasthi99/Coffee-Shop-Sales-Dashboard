☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze coffee shop sales performance, customer footfall, product demand, store performance, order characteristics, and sales trends from January 1, 2023 to June 30, 2023.

The dashboard converts raw coffee shop transaction data into an easy-to-understand business view that can help identify sales patterns, high-performing products, busy days, and store-level performance.


🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

Analyze overall coffee shop sales performance.

Track total sales and customer footfall.

Understand sales patterns across weekdays.

Identify the top-selling products.

Analyze sales contribution by product category.

Compare store locations based on sales and footfall.

Understand order-size distribution.

Analyze hourly quantity trends.

Provide insights that can support business decisions.

🔎 Key Business Insights

☕ Coffee is the leading category

Coffee contributes 38.63% of total sales, making it the largest sales category.

🍵 Tea is another major revenue contributor

Tea contributes 28.11%, meaning Coffee and Tea together account for approximately 66.74% of sales.

🕙 Morning hours are highly active

The hourly quantity trend shows strong customer activity between approximately 8 AM and 11 AM, with the highest quantity around 10 AM.

📅 Weekday performance varies

Sales are not evenly distributed throughout the week. Monday and Friday show particularly strong performance, while Saturday is comparatively lower.

🏪 Store locations have similar sales performance

The three displayed locations generate relatively close sales volumes, while their footfall levels can be compared to understand differences in average customer value.

🥤 Product-level performance is concentrated

A small group of products, led by Barista Espresso and Brewed Chai Tea, contributes a significant amount of sales.

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

Focus on peak morning hours
Ensure sufficient staff and inventory during high-demand morning periods.

Promote high-performing products
Use products such as Barista Espresso and Brewed Chai Tea in combos and promotional offers.

Increase low-performing category sales
Explore cross-selling strategies for categories with smaller sales contributions.

Analyze Saturday performance
Investigate why Saturday sales are lower and consider targeted weekend promotions.

Compare store efficiency
Evaluate sales per customer/footfall across locations instead of looking at sales alone.

Use category-level trends for inventory planning
Prioritize inventory availability for high-contribution categories such as Coffee and Tea.
